Part I. Numbers, Limits and Derivatives

Part I builds the real numbers from axioms and studies limits. Limits of sequences come first, then the topology of the real line, then limits of functions, continuity, and derivatives. It assumes the reader has taken courses in the calculus sequence, but it does not assume the reader has taken a course in proofs.
