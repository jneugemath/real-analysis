Part III. Measure and Lebesgue Integrals

Part III builds Lebesgue measure and the Lebesgue integral. Chapter 13 sets up the general language of \(\sigma\)-algebras and measures. Chapter 14 constructs Lebesgue measure on \(\mathbb{R}\), the main example. Chapters 15 and 16 define measurable functions and their integrals, and prove the convergence theorems that make the Lebesgue integral more useful than the Riemann integral. Chapter 17 connects integration and differentiation. Part III assumes Parts I and II. Sections marked with a star can be skipped. The starred Chapter 18 introduces the spaces \(L^p\).
