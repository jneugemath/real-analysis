Appendix C. Hints and Solutions

This appendix gives hints and solutions for the exercises marked [Hint] or [Solution] in the text. They appear in the same order as the exercises. Most solutions come from homework and tests in MAT 315 and MAT 815. Try each exercise before reading its hint, and use the hint before reading a solution. There is almost always more than one correct proof, so a proof that differs from the one here may still be right. Each heading links back to its exercise.
